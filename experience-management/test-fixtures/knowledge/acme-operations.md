# Acme Service Recovery Handbook

## Ownership

The Atlas service-recovery programme is owned by Ada Nwosu, Director of Customer Operations. Ada reports to Daniel Okafor, the Chief Operating Officer. The executive sponsor is Miriam Bello, Chief Executive Officer.

## Incident target

For a severity-one customer outage, the response team must acknowledge the incident within 15 minutes. A customer update must be published within 30 minutes and then every 45 minutes until service is restored.

## Approval

Refunds above NGN 250,000 require approval from the Chief Operating Officer. Refunds at or below that threshold may be approved by the Director of Customer Operations.

## Evidence retention

Incident evidence is retained for 18 months. The Compliance team owns deletion at the end of the retention period.
