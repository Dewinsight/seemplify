# Atlas Partner Responsibilities

Northstar Logistics operates the Atlas programme's last-mile delivery network. Northstar Logistics contracts Bright Route Limited to deliver parcels in Lagos.

Bright Route Limited uses the Beacon dispatch platform. The Beacon platform is maintained by Kemi Adeyemi's Platform Reliability team.

The Platform Reliability team escalates Beacon severity-one incidents to Ada Nwosu. Ada Nwosu owns the Atlas service-recovery programme.

This chain establishes that Kemi Adeyemi's team supports the platform used by the Lagos delivery subcontractor for Atlas.
