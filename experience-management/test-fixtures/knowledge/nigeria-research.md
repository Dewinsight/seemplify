# Nigeria Customer Experience Study

The study surveyed 420 active customers in Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, and Kano in June 2026. The overall response rate was 62 percent.

## Findings

Delivery predictability was the strongest driver of satisfaction. Forty-eight percent of dissatisfied respondents reported that delivery estimates changed after checkout. In Lagos, the median reported delay was two days. In Abuja, it was one day.

Customers who received a proactive delay notification were 31 percentage points more likely to say they would buy again than customers who discovered the delay themselves.

## Recommendation

The research team recommends an automatic delay-notification workflow before adding more post-delivery survey questions. The workflow should be piloted in Lagos, then evaluated after six weeks using repeat-purchase intent and complaint volume.
