Cross-border operating model

One organization, two statutory payroll boundaries

Defects found by E2E

Two product issues were fixed during acceptance

IssueFix
A prefilled account-holder name incorrectly counted as bank details and blocked saving an otherwise valid employee assignment.Banking intent now requires an actual bank/account/routing value; the employee tax assignment saves successfully.
Legal-employer identity and the preview-only warning were hidden when a run had no variable-work records.The employer snapshot and finalization warning are now always rendered as a separate run-level section.
